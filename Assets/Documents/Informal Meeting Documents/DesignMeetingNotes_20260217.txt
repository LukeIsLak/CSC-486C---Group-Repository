Present: Jonah, Nick, Nathan, Will, Justin

-- Cards --
* Elemental as justification for functionality.
* Emergently synergized - different kinds of attacks work well with each other.
* 18-19 cards will be created for this alpha.
* Card types:
 * AOE explosive projectiles - fireball (explodes on anything, burn DOT), spark (fizzles on walls, explodes on enemies, stun)
 * AOE pusher projectiles - tidal wave (wave carries enemies to wall, deals damage continuously)
 * AOE snare traps - bramble snare 
 * Player AOE - life steal
 * AOE vacuum grenades - void hole
 * Homing attacks - astral dagger (single target), lightning bolt (ricocheting / chain lightning on multiple targets within range)

-- Inventory/Deck System --
* Inventory has active deck, and a buffer.
* At the end of an encounter, any cards picked up are then shown and the game asks the player which they want to incorporate into the deck.
* If player selects new additions and has less than maximum deck size, new cards are added without issue. If exceeding maximum, also prompted to discard cards to make space.
* Card removal from active deck without replacement with another card is a privileged action that can only occur through merchants / NPCs.

-- Merchants --
* Buy cards / buy health (and mana) max increases
* Pay to have cards removed from deck

-- Mystery Encounters --
* Random selection from a pool of possible events:
 * Decrease maximum HP to discard a card of one's choosing 
 * Randomly transported to another node on the same layer of map (that node is marked as cleared, and player progresses down)
 * Pay gold to increase maximum health
 * Pay with health to gain gold (maximum stays the same)
 * Unguarded cache of gold
 * Unguarded random card
 * Combat encounter (lol)

-- Player Incentives --
* Traversing the map in specific ways:
 * Going to shops to get rid of cards, buy new cards, recover health
 * Going to rest points to recover health
 * Going to mystery encounters to potentially get new loot
* Strategies for combat encounters:
 * Dungeons can be cleared without defeating all enemies - there's an "exit door."
 * Rushing through to exit without killing anything = clear node, but no rewards gained and encounters only get harder.
 * One reward per each: Minimum kill threshold, complete wipe of encounter, both prior criteria under time limit.
 * Specific groups of enemies are tied to chests in dungeons, once they've all been killed the chest opens (RoR2 Void Seed)
 * Enemies drop gold on kill?

-- Questboard --
* Minimum three options for dungeon quests.
* Information visible: "difficulty," gold payout.
* Difficulty characterized through skull ratings, 1-5, determined based on number of layers and number/frequency of rest points.
